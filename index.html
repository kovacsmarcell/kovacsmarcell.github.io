01  /  About

Six disciplines, one researcher

I am a computer engineer who became a political scientist who became an economist, not by switching tracks, but by refusing to pick one.

My work sits where code, capital, and policy collide: how technology firms grow from startup to scaleup to unicorn, what states do to help or hinder them, and how markets price it all. Most of it is about semiconductors and AI.

I research, write, build, and represent. I publish with ITIF in Washington and in Közgazdasági Szemle, and I work on the same questions in practice, from enterprise software to mentoring a deep-tech startup and leading a foundation.
